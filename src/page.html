      Sources

Verses: King James Version; biblical names checked against Strong's Hebrew lexicon.

      Seat Method

      Seats use the Bader–Ofer (D'Hondt) method among lists above 3.25%, with the five 2026 surplus-vote agreements: Likud–Religious Zionism, Shas–United Torah Judaism, Yashar–The Democrats, Together–Yisrael Beiteinu and Joint List–Ra'am. Projections before the count ends are not official; the Elections Committee certifies seats on Nov 4.

      Camps And Red Lines

      Camps reflect each party's declared alignment before the vote. Red lines are simplified from public statements in the campaign. Both update after the President's consultations.
